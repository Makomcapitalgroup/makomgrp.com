Our Position

We are not what you probably expect to find here.

Panama's real estate market has no shortage of agents who facilitate transactions and providers who execute services. What's almost nonexistent is a company that understands a property is not a transaction: it is an asset with a life cycle of its own.

MAKOM CAPITAL GROUP was born from that observation. We bring together real estate brokerage and property management not because it is a good business idea, but because it is the only honest way to support an owner, investor, or company for as long as they hold that property.

We do not close the deal and walk away. We stay. That is what makes a steward of wealth, not an agent. And that is the difference that defines everything we do.

Every space has a purpose waiting to be discovered. MAKOM finds it, protects it, and develops it.

Why MAKOM

What makes a steward of wealth different.

Integrated from day one, not merged later

MAKOM isn't the result of joining a broker with a maintenance provider. It was born integrated: its founders bring dual expertise from day one. The advisor who negotiates your property understands what it costs to maintain it. And the team that maintains it understands its commercial value.

The contract isn't the end

In most real estate transactions, signing the contract is the last point of contact with the agent. At MAKOM, it's the first. Our relationship with clients is built in the time that follows the closing — not the time before it.

Local knowledge, international standards

We understand the Panamanian market with the depth that only years of practice in the territory can give. At the same time, we build the company under the management standards of leading international real estate groups.

One company for the entire life cycle

You don't need to coordinate five different providers to manage a property. MAKOM covers everything from the initial search to long-term maintenance. One point of contact. One standard. No gaps between stages.
